Privacy Policy for Drop 100 Floors

This Privacy Policy explains how Drop 100 Floors handles information when you use the app. We designed the app to be simple and privacy-friendly.

Summary: Drop 100 Floors does not require an account, does not show ads, does not include in-app purchases, does not use third-party analytics SDKs, and does not operate its own backend server.

1. Information We Collect

Drop 100 Floors does not collect personal information directly from users.

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

2. Local Game Data

The app may store gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as:

  • Your selected language
  • Music, sound effect, and haptic feedback settings
  • Your selected theme
  • Your best score
  • Your recent game records

This data is stored on your device and is used only to provide the app's gameplay and settings experience. If you delete the app, local data may be deleted as well.

3. Apple Game Center

Drop 100 Floors may use Apple Game Center to submit your best score to Apple Game Center leaderboards.

Game Center is provided by Apple. Any information processed through Game Center is handled according to Apple's own privacy practices. You can manage Game Center settings through your Apple ID and device settings.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Drop 100 Floors does not display ads and does not use advertising SDKs.

The app does not use tracking technologies for advertising purposes and does not request permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

5. Analytics

Drop 100 Floors does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store, sales, performance, and crash information through Apple developer tools. This information is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Because Drop 100 Floors does not directly collect personal information, we do not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

7. Children's Privacy

Drop 100 Floors does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include user accounts, chat, advertising, or user-generated content features.

8. International Availability

Drop 100 Floors may be available in multiple countries and regions. The app is designed to avoid collecting personal information directly from users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
